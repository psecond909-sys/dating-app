Lovira Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 4, 2026

Lovira ("Lovira", "we", "us", or "our") is a mobile dating application that allows users to create profiles, discover other users, like profiles, match with other users, and communicate through the application.

This Privacy Policy explains what information Lovira collects, how we use it, how it may be shared, how we protect it, and the choices available to users.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use Lovira, we may collect the following information:

  • Account information: name, email address, password-related account information, date of birth, and user ID.
  • Profile information: profile photographs and information that you choose to add to your dating profile.
  • Location information: approximate or device-derived location information when you grant the application permission to access location. Location may be used to help show relevant nearby profiles.
  • Likes and matches: information about profiles you like, users who like you, matches, and related interactions.
  • Messages: messages you send or receive through Lovira's messaging features.
  • Blocking information: information about users you block or other safety-related actions you take in the application.
  • Purchase information: information necessary to provide and manage premium or in-app purchase features. Payment card information is processed by the applicable payment platform and is not stored directly by Lovira.
  • Device and usage information: information that may be provided by third-party services used by the application, including advertising and crash or service-related information.

2. How We Use Information

We may use information to:

  • Create and manage your Lovira account.
  • Create and display your dating profile.
  • Allow you to discover and interact with other users.
  • Provide likes, matches, messaging, blocking, and related features.
  • Use location information to provide location-related dating features when permission is granted.
  • Provide premium and in-app purchase features.
  • Display advertisements to users who are not entitled to an ad-free premium experience.
  • Maintain, secure, troubleshoot, and improve Lovira.
  • Prevent abuse, fraud, spam, and violations of our rules.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

3. Firebase and Cloud Services

Lovira uses Firebase services provided by Google to support features such as authentication, database storage, and cloud storage.

Depending on the feature being used, information such as account information, profile information, photographs, likes, matches, messages, and other application data may be stored using these services.

4. Advertising

Lovira may use Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) or other advertising services to display advertisements.

Advertising providers may collect information such as device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP address, approximate location, and information about interactions with advertisements, subject to their own privacy policies and applicable settings and laws.

Premium features may provide an ad-free experience where applicable.

5. In-App Purchases

Lovira may offer premium subscriptions or other in-app purchases. Transactions are handled through the applicable app-store payment system. Lovira does not directly collect or store your full payment card number.

6. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information as a product.

Information may be shared with service providers when necessary to operate Lovira. These may include providers for authentication, cloud storage, databases, advertising, app-store purchases, security, and technical services.

We may also disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with law, legal process, protect users, investigate abuse or fraud, enforce our terms, or protect the rights and safety of Lovira and its users.

7. Information Visible to Other Users

Information that you choose to place in your Lovira dating profile, including your name, photographs, and other profile information, may be visible to other Lovira users as part of the dating experience.

You should not place passwords, financial information, government identification numbers, or other highly sensitive information in your public profile.

8. Messages and User Interactions

Messages sent through Lovira may be stored so that messaging and matching features can operate. Users should avoid sending sensitive personal, financial, or confidential information through chat.

9. Location Information

If you grant Lovira permission to access your device location, we may use location information to support location-related dating features.

You can manage location permission through your device settings. Disabling location permission may affect features that depend on location.

10. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

However, no internet transmission or electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Data Retention

We retain account and profile information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide Lovira and its features, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and maintain security.

When an account is deleted, we will delete or anonymize associated personal information within a reasonable period, subject to information that we are legally required or permitted to retain.

12. Account Deletion

Users can request deletion of their Lovira account and associated personal data through the account-deletion functionality provided by Lovira or by contacting us through the support contact provided below.

When a valid deletion request is received, we will process the request and delete or anonymize associated personal information, subject to applicable legal requirements and legitimate retention obligations.

13. Children's Privacy

Lovira is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 18. We do not knowingly allow persons under 18 to create or use Lovira accounts.

If we learn that a person under 18 has provided personal information or created an account, we will take reasonable steps to remove the information and account.

14. Third-Party Services

Lovira may use third-party services including Google Firebase, Google Mobile Ads, Google Play billing services, and other service providers necessary to operate the application.

These third parties may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

15. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When changes are made, we will update the "Last updated" date shown at the top of this page.

16. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, privacy practices, or account/data deletion, please contact the Lovira support team through the support contact provided in the Lovira application or its Google Play listing.

17. Consent

By creating or using a Lovira account, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy and that your information may be processed as described here, subject to applicable law.
