Lovira Account Deletion

Lovira respects your privacy and provides a way for users to request deletion of their Lovira account and associated personal data.

How to Request Account Deletion

  1. Open the Lovira app.
  2. Sign in to your Lovira account.
  3. Open your account/profile settings.
  4. Select the account deletion option.
  5. Follow the instructions shown in the app to confirm deletion.

What Data Is Deleted

When an account deletion request is completed, Lovira will delete the account and associated personal data that is eligible for deletion, including profile information, profile photos, likes, matches, and other account-associated data.

Messages and Other Data

Information associated with the account may also be deleted where applicable. Some information may be retained for a limited period when necessary for security, fraud prevention, legal compliance, dispute resolution, or other legitimate legal purposes.

Retention

Data that must be retained for legal, security, or legitimate business purposes will be kept only for the period necessary for that purpose and then deleted or anonymized where applicable.

Need Help?

If you cannot access your account or cannot complete the deletion process, use the support contact provided in the Lovira app or on the Google Play listing.
